Baiyen Music Player is an offline, on-device player for MP3 files that the user imports. No account or sign-in is required, and the app makes no network requests.

The user interface is in Traditional Chinese. Labels used below:
加入範例歌曲 = Add sample songs | 匯入 MP3 = Import MP3
音樂庫 = Library | 播放清單 = Playlists | 建立清單 = Create playlist
完成 = Done
Playback controls at the bottom are icons: previous, play/pause, next. A playlist page has icons for add/remove songs (+), restart from the first song, and play/pause.

HOW TO TEST
1. On first launch the library is empty. Tap 加入範例歌曲 (Add sample songs) to add two bundled royalty-free test tones. You can also import your own MP3 files with 匯入 MP3 (Import MP3) from the Files app; multiple selection is supported.
2. Tap a song title to start playback. Drag the progress bar to seek, and use the previous/next icons to switch tracks. Pressing previous more than 5 seconds into a song restarts it.
3. Background audio: while a song is playing, lock the device or switch to another app. Playback continues, and the song can be paused and resumed from the Lock Screen. The app declares the "audio" background mode only for this purpose.
4. Playlists: tap 播放清單 (Playlists), enter a name and tap 建立清單 (Create playlist); the new playlist opens. Tap the + icon, select songs and tap 完成 (Done). Tap the play icon to play the playlist from the first song, or tap a song to start from it. Long-press a song and drag it to reorder. In the playlist list, the pencil icon renames a playlist and the trash icon deletes it.

PRIVACY
The app collects no data. Imported files and playlist information are stored only in the app's container on the device. The app does not use encryption beyond what iOS provides.
